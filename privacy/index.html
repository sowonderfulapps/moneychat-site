Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

MoneyChat is an expense tracker for iOS and Android. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who else processes it, and how to get it erased. It describes what the app actually does — not what it might do one day.

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is SoWonderfulApps LLC. For any question about this policy or your data, write to hi@moneychat.pro.

What we collect, and why

Your recordsAmounts, categories, dates, notes, the accounts and currencies you create. This is the service itself: without it the app has nothing to show you.
AccountWhen you first open the app we create a guest account with a generated internal address — no personal data is needed. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google we store the email address they return (which may be Apple's private relay address), and the name and photo if the provider supplies them. This exists so your records survive a new phone.
Device and settingsA push token, the platform, your interface language and your time zone. The time zone is what lets the evening reminder arrive at 20:00 where you are rather than somewhere else.
Receipt photosWhen you scan a receipt, the image is sent over an encrypted connection, recognised, and split into entries. The image itself is not stored on our servers — only the entries you confirm are saved.
Usage and stabilityAggregate events and crash reports, used to find what is broken and what nobody can find. We do not use them to build a profile of you.
PaymentsSubscriptions are purchased through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details — we only receive whether a subscription is active.

Bank notifications on Android

Android can offer to pre-fill an expense from a bank's payment notification. This is off by default and only works if you switch it on. The notification text is parsed on your device, nothing is saved without your confirmation, and the feature can be switched off at any time. The app works fully without it.

Advertising

The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad when you switch between accounts, delivered by Google AdMob. A Pro subscription removes advertising entirely. On iOS, personalised advertising is only used if you allow tracking in the system prompt; if you decline, ads are non-personalised.

Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — storing and showing your records, syncing them across your devices, processing subscriptions.
  • Legitimate interest — keeping the service stable and secure, aggregate usage statistics, preventing abuse.
  • Consent — push notifications, tracking for personalised advertising, and reading bank notifications on Android. Each can be withdrawn in the app or in system settings.

Who else processes your data

We use a small number of providers, each for one job:

  • Railway — hosting of the server and the database.
  • Google Cloud (Vision, Vertex AI) — recognising the text of a receipt and splitting it into entries. The image is processed and discarded.
  • Firebase (Google) — push notifications and aggregate analytics.
  • Adapty — subscription status.
  • Google AdMob — advertising in the free version.
  • Apple and Google — sign-in and payment processing.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone outside this list. Some of these providers operate outside the European Economic Area; such transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Your records are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, the account, all transactions and all wallets are permanently erased from our servers — this is a real deletion, not a hidden flag. Backups are overwritten on their own schedule within 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app: the person icon in the chat header → Delete account at the bottom of the screen. If you no longer have the app installed, write to hi@moneychat.pro from the address you signed in with, and we will erase the account for you. If you signed in with Apple and chose to hide your email, tell us the relay address you were given — we cannot find you by your real one.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, have it erased, object to processing, or ask for it in a portable form. Write to the address above; we answer within 30 days. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to the data protection authority of your country of residence.

Children

MoneyChat is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

If the app changes hands

If MoneyChat is sold or transferred to another company, your data may be transferred to the new controller so that your records keep working. We will tell you before that happens, and you will be able to delete your account first.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.
