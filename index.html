Three seconds, start to finish

The main screen is a chat. There is nothing to set up before the first entry, and no account to create.

01

Type the amount

Open the app and type 15 on the keypad. That is the whole input.

02

Send it

The expense is logged and a category is picked for you — and the app learns from your corrections.

03

Scroll back any time

Your spending history reads like a conversation. Tap an entry to change the amount, category or date.

What else is inside

Accounts and currencies

Card, cash and savings as separate accounts, switched with one tap. Each has its own currency, balances convert at current rates, and a transfer between two accounts is recorded once and shows on both sides.

Receipt scanner

Photograph a receipt and it is split into entries by category — groceries, household, drinks. Correct any line, drop what you don't need, or save the whole receipt as one expense.

Analytics in plain words

Where the money goes, month over month, for one account or all of them converted into a single currency. A short summary first, the details only if you want them.

Private by default

Start without creating an account. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional and only carries your data to another device. Deleting your account erases everything from our servers — it is not hidden, it is gone.

Support

Something broken, a question about a subscription, or a request about your data — write to hi@moneychat.pro. We read everything and usually reply within two working days.
